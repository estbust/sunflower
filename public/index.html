Learning Pathways

Programs for growing children

We care for children from 19 months through 8 years in classrooms with routines and activities suited to their age and stage.

The Bee Room • 19–36 Months

Toddler Care: The Bee Room

A warm, gentle environment in the Bee Room (19 months - 36 months) where toddlers explore sensory play, build early communication skills, and practice independence through supportive daily routines.

  • The Bee Room: nurturing sensory haven for 19 to 36 months
  • Sensory texture and water discovery
  • Nurturing social and emotional transitions
  • Motor skills and balance play
Learn More →
Ladybug & Snail • 3–5 Years

Preschool: Ladybug & Snail Rooms

Divided into the Ladybug Room (3-year-olds) and the Snail Room (Older 3-year-olds and younger 4-year-olds) to support each developmental stage, children explore art, stories, friendships, letters, numbers, and tracing to prepare for kindergarten.

  • The Ladybug Room (3s) & Snail Room (Older 3s & younger 4s)
  • Letters, numbers, and tracing practice
  • Circle time
  • Creative art and cooperative play
Learn More →
The Dragonfly Room • 5–8 Years

Pre-K & School-Age Care: The Dragonfly Room

The Dragonfly Room (Pre-K and School age, 5 to 8 years) offers a safe, welcoming space for before and after school, full days, and school breaks where children unwind, do homework, make art, and play with friends.

  • The Dragonfly Room: dedicated Pre-K & elementary care (5–8 yrs)
  • Guided homework and quiet study
  • Art, crafts, and board games
  • Team athletics and outdoor games

Admissions Journey

How Our Admissions Process Works

We take time to get to know your child and your family values to ensure a positive, harmonious educational experience.

1 Step 1: Online Inquiry

Submit an online enrollment inquiry sharing your contact information and general schedule preferences. We never ask for medical details or full birthdates on our online forms.

2 Step 2: Campus Visit

Join us for a guided campus tour. You will meet our teaching team, observe classrooms in session, and experience our outdoor play spaces.

3 Step 3: Program Fit Review

Our admissions team meets with your family to discuss schedule alignment, developmental readiness, and classroom cohort compatibility.

Admissions Notice: Submitting an online inquiry or scheduling a campus tour initiates a conversation with our admissions team to explore program fit and availability. It does not guarantee admission or reserve an open space in any cohort.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for Anchorage Families

Find essential details about our Anchorage campus location, program age groups, tour visits, and digital child privacy safeguards.

We serve children ages 19 months through 8 years across dedicated classrooms: Toddler Care in The Bee Room (19–36 months), Preschool in the Ladybug & Snail Rooms (3–5 years), and Pre-K & School-Age Care in The Dragonfly Room (5–8 years).

Our campus is located at 2500 Boniface Parkway, Anchorage, AK 99504, offering convenient access from across the Anchorage bowl with nature-rich outdoor play spaces.

You can schedule a tour by completing our Contact & Tours form or calling our campus directly at (907) 277-3005. Tours are scheduled on weekdays between 10:00 AM and 11:00 AM, and in the afternoon from 2:00 PM to 10:00 PM.

To safeguard children's privacy, our web forms strictly reject medical details, allergies, clinical diagnoses, and full calendar birthdates. All confidential health records are completed on private paper documentation after an enrollment agreement is reached.

Submitting an inquiry initiates a conversation with our admissions team to explore program fit and check schedule availability. It does not guarantee admission or reserve an open space in any cohort.

Sunflower Preschool is open Monday through Friday from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM, welcoming full-time and part-time preschool schedules as well as before- and after-school care.
