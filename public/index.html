Admissions Journey

How Our Admissions Process Works

We take time to get to know your child and your family values to ensure a positive, harmonious educational experience.

1 Step 1: Online Inquiry

Submit an online enrollment inquiry sharing your contact information and general schedule preferences. We never ask for medical details or full birthdates on our online forms.

2 Step 2: Campus Visit

Join us for a guided campus tour. You will meet our teaching team, observe classrooms in session, and experience our outdoor nature learning garden.

3 Step 3: Program Fit Review

Our admissions team meets with your family to discuss schedule alignment, developmental readiness, and classroom cohort compatibility.

Admissions Notice: Submitting an online inquiry or scheduling a campus tour initiates a conversation with our admissions team to explore program fit and availability. It does not guarantee admission or reserve an open space in any cohort.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for Anchorage Families

Find essential details about our Anchorage campus location, program age groups, tour visits, and digital child privacy safeguards.

We serve children ages 18 months through 10 years across three dedicated cohorts: Toddler Care (18–36 months), Preschool (3–5 years), and School-Age Care (5–10 years).

Our campus is located at 2500 Boniface Parkway, Anchorage, AK 99504, offering convenient access from across the Anchorage bowl with nature-rich outdoor play spaces.

You can schedule a tour by completing our Contact & Tours form or calling our campus directly at (907) 277-3005. Tours are scheduled weekday mornings between 9:30 AM and 11:00 AM.

To safeguard children's privacy, our web forms strictly reject medical details, allergies, clinical diagnoses, and full calendar birthdates. All confidential health records are completed on private paper documentation after an enrollment agreement is reached.

Submitting an inquiry initiates a conversation with our admissions team to explore program fit and check schedule availability. It does not guarantee admission or reserve an open space in any cohort.

Sunflower Preschool is open Monday through Friday from 7:30 AM to 5:30 PM, welcoming full-time and part-time preschool schedules as well as before- and after-school care.
