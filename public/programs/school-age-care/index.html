Ages 5 to 10 Years

School-Age Care Program

A dynamic after-school and seasonal community where elementary students decompress, pursue maker passions, receive homework mentorship, and stay physically active.

Program Pillars

Enrichment Beyond the Traditional School Day

Elementary children need a balanced environment that respects their growing independence while offering supportive educator mentorship.

Study Support

Homework Mentorship & Reading Club

A calm, organized space where students can complete assignments with accessible reference books and teacher guidance, freeing up valuable family time in the evening.

  • Quiet study tables with low distractions
  • One-on-one help with reading comprehension and math assignments
  • Comfortable book lounge with diverse chapter books and graphic novels
Innovation

STEAM Maker Lab & Creative Engineering

Students engage in self-directed creative projects. They design architectural models, construct simple machines, weave textiles, and explore coding fundamentals.

  • Hands-on woodworking and cardboard engineering tools
  • Circuits, robotics kits, and mechanical contraptions
  • Process art studio, textile craft, and multimedia design
Active Movement

Outdoor Recreation & Team Athletics

After sitting in elementary classrooms, children need ample time to run, stretch, and play. Our sports fields and play court encourage healthy physical vitality.

  • Cooperative sports including kickball, soccer, and relay games
  • Nature trails and climbing obstacle challenges
  • Free-form active play and playground adventures
Character

Youth Leadership & Peer Collaboration

Older children take pride in peer mentoring and community service projects. We support leadership, ethical decision making, and inclusive teamwork.

  • Student-led interest clubs and board game tournaments
  • Community service initiatives such as campus garden stewardship
  • Guided peer conflict resolution and team problem solving
Flexible Enrollment Tracks

Care Options for Elementary Families

We offer consistent care schedules designed around local Anchorage elementary school calendars and working family routines.

🕒 After-School Care Track

Monday through Friday from school dismissal until 5:30 PM. Includes nutritious afternoon snack, supervised homework time, daily outdoor play, and rotating maker lab workshops.

📅 Seasonal & Full-Day Camp Tracks

Full-day enrichment care during school holidays, teacher professional development days, and summer breaks. Features extended project investigations, hiking, and campus festivals.

After-School Rhythm

A Typical Afternoon in School-Age Care

Elementary children thrive with a predictable after-school balance of unwinding, physical games, focused study, and open creative invention.

3:00 PM – 3:30 PM
Arrival, Nourishing Snack & Decompression

Welcome greeting, healthy afternoon refreshments, personal cubby check-in, and social conversation.

3:30 PM – 4:15 PM
Homework Mentorship & Reading Hub

Quiet study tables, educator math guidance, reading check-ins, and peer partner study.

4:15 PM – 5:00 PM
STEAM Maker Lab, Woodcraft & Creative Studios

Robotics kits, process art, cardboard prototyping, textile arts, and student-led interest clubs.

5:00 PM – 5:30 PM
Outdoor Recreation, Cooperative Games & Departure

Soccer, kickball, running games on the play meadow, nature challenges, and family pickup.
