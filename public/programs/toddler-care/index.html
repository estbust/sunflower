Ages 19 to 36 Months

Toddler Care Program

A gentle, nurturing bridge between home and the wider world. Our toddler environment supports blossoming independence, sensory discovery, and responsive social bonds.

Developmental Focus

Supporting Growth at Every Milestone

Between 19 and 36 months, children experience extraordinary strides in language, motor coordination, and self-awareness. Our curriculum is tailored to embrace these rapid leaps.

Communication

Emergent Language & Expression

Toddlers build vocabulary through daily interactive read-alouds, conversational turn-taking, rhyming songs, and expressive fingerplays. Our educators speak with warmth, validating every gesture and emerging word.

  • Rich, descriptive verbal modeling
  • Storytelling with felt boards and puppets
  • Simple sign language to bridge early communication
Discovery

Sensory Exploration & Motor Skills

Hands-on sensory encounters form the bedrock of toddler cognition. Children manipulate natural textures, explore water flow, pour sand, and strengthen balance across soft obstacles.

  • Low climbing platforms and gentle indoor turf ramps
  • Water, grain, and non-toxic dough exploration
  • Fine-motor sorting with wooden rings and nesting bowls
Emotional Well-Being

Social Trust & Gentle Routines

Predictable daily rhythms create emotional security. Toddlers learn to navigate first peer relationships, practicing gentle touches, parallel play, and taking turns with educator support.

  • Consistent primary caregivers who understand each child
  • Comfort nooks with plush floor pillows for quiet recharging
  • Empathetic guidance during emotional big-feeling moments
Self-Help

Autonomy & Practical Life

We celebrate a toddler's desire to “do it myself!” Low furniture, child-sized cubbies, and simple self-care routines foster confidence and pride in their capabilities.

  • Hanging their coats and placing boots in low cubbies
  • Washing hands at toddler-height accessible sinks
  • Self-guided selection of accessible shelf activities
The Learning Space

A Thoughtfully Prepared Toddler Environment

Every inch of our toddler space is scaled to a child's perspective, emphasizing natural wood, warm ambient lighting, and open pathways for unhindered mobility.

🌱 Low Accessible Shelving

Materials are displayed at eye-level in open wooden baskets so toddlers can choose, use, and return items independently.

💧 Sensory Water & Nature Hub

Dedicated water basins, herb planters, and smooth river stones give toddlers immediate, direct interaction with natural elements.

☀️ Toddler Outdoor Meadow

A protected outdoor space with soft grass, shallow slopes, sensory gardens, and shade trees designed specifically for toddler steps.

Daily Rhythms

A Gentle Daily Schedule for Toddlers

Predictable rhythms give toddlers the security to explore freely, knowing each transition unfolds with patience, care, and familiarity.

7:00 AM – 8:15 AM
Free choice / table activities

Children choose from available table activities as they arrive.

8:15 AM – 8:30 AM
Get ready for breakfast

Put away activities, wash hands, and move to the breakfast area.

8:30 AM – 9:00 AM
Breakfast

Eat breakfast and practice mealtime routines.

9:00 AM – 9:15 AM
Circle time

Gather for a brief group meeting, songs, or conversation.

9:15 AM – 9:45 AM
Social and emotional learning

Group activity focused on social and emotional learning.

9:45 AM – 10:30 AM
Get ready for outside play

Use the bathroom or have diapering as needed, get dressed for the weather, and gather outdoor gear.

10:40 AM – 11:00 AM
Outside

Outdoor play and movement.
