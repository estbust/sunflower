The Ladybug & Snail Rooms • Ages 3 to 5 Years

Preschool: Ladybug & Snail Rooms

An inspiring, playful environment tailored to developmental milestones. Our preschool program is thoughtfully divided into two classrooms to better support each stage: the Ladybug Room (for 3-year-olds) and the Snail Room (for older 3-year-olds and younger 4-year-olds).

Integrated Curriculum

Hands-On Learning Centers & Discovery

Preschoolers flourish when given the autonomy to investigate big ideas. Our classrooms feature interactive interest studios where learning happens naturally through play.

Literacy & Communication

Early Language & Emergent Reading

We immerse children in an environment where letters, words, and stories come to life. Children dictate original tales, dramatize books, and build phonemic awareness through rhythm and rhymes.

  • Daily interactive read-alouds with open discussion
  • Letter recognition and phonetic sound games
  • Child-dictated storytelling
Numbers & Tracing

Numbers, Shapes & Tracing

Children practice recognizing letters and numbers, tracing shapes and lines, and exploring early counting and patterns in playful, supportive daily moments.

  • Number recognition, counting games, and geometric shapes
  • Hand-eye coordination, pencil grip, and guided tracing
  • Hands-on block building and cooperative play
Creativity

Expressive Arts

Our art studio invites children to experiment with clays, watercolors, and recycled materials. Process art takes center stage, honoring personal imagination over rigid templates.

  • Clay sculpting and natural pottery techniques
  • Color mixing, easel painting, and collage
  • Music-making, rhythmic instruments, and movement
Community

Social-Emotional Readiness

Preschoolers learn to navigate peer friendships, express their feelings constructively, and collaborate on shared projects. We foster empathy and self-regulation as core life skills.

  • Collaborative problem solving during group challenges
  • Mindful calming strategies and emotional vocabulary
  • Democratic morning meetings where every voice counts
Classroom Routine

A Day in the Preschool Classroom

Our preschool schedule balances intentional educator-guided inquiry with child-led project work and abundant Alaskan outdoor play.

7:00 AM – 8:20 AM
Free choice / quiet table activities

Children choose a quiet activity, such as puzzles, books, drawing, or table toys, as they arrive.

8:20 AM – 8:30 AM
Get ready for breakfast

Put away activities and move to the breakfast area.

8:30 AM – 9:00 AM
Breakfast

Eat breakfast and practice mealtime routines.

9:00 AM – 9:15 AM
Circle time and story reading

Gather for a short group meeting, sing a song or talk about the day, then read a story together.

9:15 AM – 9:45 AM
Centers

Choose from sensory play, dramatic play, puzzles, writing, and blocks.

9:45 AM – 10:10 AM
Fine motor skills

Trace the letter of the week.

10:10 AM – 10:30 AM
Get ready for outside

Use the bathroom as needed, put on outdoor clothing, and line up.

10:30 AM – 11:00 AM
Outside

Outdoor play and movement.

11:15 AM – 11:30 AM
Music

Music activity or group singing.

11:30 AM – 11:40 AM
Wash hands

Wash hands and prepare for lunch.

11:40 AM – 12:30 PM
Lunch

Eat lunch and follow mealtime routines.

12:30 PM – 2:00 PM
Nap

Rest time.

2:00 PM – 2:15 PM
Potty

Bathroom routine after nap.

2:15 PM – 2:45 PM
Art / science

Small-group activity.

2:45 PM – 3:00 PM
Get ready for snack

Clean up and wash hands.

3:00 PM – 3:15 PM
Snack

Eat snack.

3:15 PM – 3:45 PM
Math

Small-group lesson around the number of the week.

3:45 PM – 4:00 PM
Board games

Play board games together and practice taking turns.

4:00 PM – 4:30 PM
Potty and get ready for outside play

Use the bathroom and get ready to go outside.

4:30 PM – 5:00 PM
Outside play

Outdoor play and movement.

5:00 PM – 6:00 PM
Free play

Children choose from available play activities.
