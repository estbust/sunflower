Ages 3 to 5 Years

Preschool Program

An inspiring, inquiry-led curriculum that harnesses children’s natural curiosity. We weave early literacy, math, scientific wonder, and creative arts into rich daily play.

Integrated Curriculum

Hands-On Learning Centers & Discovery

Preschoolers flourish when given the autonomy to investigate big ideas. Our classrooms feature interactive interest studios where learning happens naturally through play.

Literacy & Communication

Early Language & Emergent Reading

We immerse children in a print-rich environment where letters, words, and stories come to life. Children dictate original tales, dramatize books, and build phonemic awareness through rhythm and rhymes.

  • Daily interactive read-alouds with open discussion
  • Letter recognition and phonetic sound games
  • Child-dictated storytelling and bookmaking studio
Math & Science

Mathematical Thinking & STEM

Math is experienced concretely through sorting, weighing, building, and pattern-making. Our science investigations encourage children to pose hypotheses, test ideas, and document outcomes.

  • Architectural block building and balance exploration
  • Counting, geometric sorting, and pattern sequencing
  • Microscope observations, nature collections, and simple experiments
Creativity

Atelier Studio & Expressive Arts

Our art studio invites children to experiment with clays, watercolors, charcoal, and recycled materials. Process art takes center stage, honoring personal imagination over rigid templates.

  • Clay sculpting and natural pottery techniques
  • Color mixing, easel painting, and collage
  • Music-making, rhythmic instruments, and movement
Community

Social-Emotional Readiness

Preschoolers learn to navigate peer friendships, express their feelings constructively, and collaborate on shared projects. We foster empathy and self-regulation as core life skills.

  • Collaborative problem solving during group challenges
  • Mindful calming strategies and emotional vocabulary
  • Democratic morning meetings where every voice counts
Future Ready

Our Kindergarten Readiness Philosophy

True readiness for elementary school is not about rote memorization or drills. It is about nurturing confident learners who know how to ask questions, work with peers, and persist through challenges.

💡 Intellectual Curiosity

Children develop an eagerness to learn, experimenting fearlessly and finding joy in discovering solutions through trial and reflection.

🤝 Collaborative Empathy

Preschoolers practice listening to classmates, sharing perspectives, and working cooperatively in diverse small groups.

🎒 Personal Self-Reliance

Children build independence in organizing their personal items, managing self-care routines, and advocating for their needs with educators.

Classroom Rhythm

A Day in the Preschool Classroom

Our preschool schedule balances intentional educator-guided inquiry with child-led project work and abundant Alaskan outdoor play.

7:00 AM – 8:20 AM
Free choice / quiet table activities

Children choose a quiet activity, such as puzzles, books, drawing, or table toys, as they arrive.

8:20 AM – 8:30 AM
Get ready for breakfast

Put away activities and move to the breakfast area.

8:30 AM – 9:00 AM
Breakfast

Eat breakfast and practice mealtime routines.

9:00 AM – 9:15 AM
Circle time and story reading

Gather for a short group meeting, sing a song or talk about the day, then read a story together.

9:15 AM – 9:45 AM
Centers

Choose from sensory play, dramatic play, puzzles, writing, and blocks.

9:45 AM – 10:10 AM
Fine motor skills

Trace the letter of the week.

10:10 AM – 10:30 AM
Get ready for outside

Use the bathroom as needed, put on outdoor clothing, and line up.

10:30 AM – 11:00 AM
Outside

Outdoor play and movement.

11:15 AM – 11:30 AM
Music

Music activity or group singing.

11:30 AM – 11:40 AM
Wash hands

Wash hands and prepare for lunch.

11:40 AM – 12:30 PM
Lunch

Eat lunch and follow mealtime routines.

12:30 PM – 2:00 PM
Nap

Rest time.

2:00 PM – 2:15 PM
Potty

Bathroom routine after nap.

2:15 PM – 2:45 PM
Art / science

Small-group activity.

2:45 PM – 3:00 PM
Get ready for snack

Clean up and wash hands.

3:00 PM – 3:15 PM
Snack

Eat snack.

3:15 PM – 3:45 PM
Math

Small-group lesson around the number of the week.

3:45 PM – 4:00 PM
Board games

Play board games together and practice taking turns.

4:00 PM – 4:30 PM
Potty and get ready for outside play

Use the bathroom and get ready to go outside.

4:30 PM – 5:00 PM
Outside play

Outdoor play and movement.

5:00 PM – 6:00 PM
Free play

Children choose from available play activities.
