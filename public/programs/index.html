Developmental Pathways

Early Learning & Care Programs

Our programs are thoughtfully designed to meet children at their distinct developmental stages, encouraging curiosity, confidence, and community through play-based pedagogy.

Three Dedicated Cohorts

A Continuous Journey from Toddlerhood to School Age

Each cohort offers tailored classroom spaces, educator ratios, and daily rhythms designed specifically for the emotional and physical needs of that age group.

Early Learning

Toddler Care

A warm, sensory-rich haven where toddlers build trust, practice early autonomy, and explore the joy of language through songs, stories, and guided play.

  • Responsive primary caregiving
  • Sensory discovery and tactile materials
  • Gross motor balance & play
  • Emergent speech & social transition

Preschool Phase

Preschool Program

An engaging preschool experience fostering scientific inquiry, early literacy, expressive studio arts, and collaborative peer problem-solving.

  • Project-based inquiries & questions
  • Pre-literacy & story circles
  • Early math patterns & counting
  • Outdoor garden & ecosystem discovery

School-Age

School-Age Care

Enriching after-school and seasonal care designed for elementary learners to unwind, explore STEM hobbies, finish homework, and enjoy active games.

  • Homework support & quiet study
  • Makerspace construction & robotics
  • Outdoor recreation & team sports
  • Leadership & peer collaboration

What Sets Us Apart

Included in Every Sunflower Program

All-Inclusive Nutritious Meals

Breakfast, lunch, and healthy snacks are freshly prepared and included in every program—saving parents time every morning.

Dedicated Potty Training

We partner closely with families to provide patient, positive, and consistent potty training support as part of our daily routine.

Uncompromising Safety

From secure facility access to low child-to-teacher ratios, we maintain a protected, nurturing space where children thrive with peace of mind.

School-Age Care Rhythm

A supportive afternoon continuum for elementary students balancing school relaxation, homework, and hands-on makerspace projects.

2:45 PM – 3:30 PM
Arrival, Check-in & Energizing Snack

Transition from the school day with wholesome snacks and friendly peer check-ins.

3:30 PM – 4:15 PM
Homework Support & Quiet Reading

Dedicated educator guidance for homework tasks, reading practice, or quiet independent research.

4:15 PM – 5:00 PM
STEAM Maker Lab & Creative Arts

Hands-on engineering challenges, robotics kits, woodworking crafts, and creative studio art.

5:00 PM – 5:30 PM
Outdoor Sports & Cooperative Team Play

Field games, relay challenges, group recreation, and evening departure preparation.
