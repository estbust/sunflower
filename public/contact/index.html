Visit Our Campus

What to Expect During a Campus Visit

We welcome families to experience our warm learning environments firsthand during active morning exploration hours.

Campus Schedule & Visiting Guidelines

Sunflower Preschool Campus
2500 Boniface Parkway, Anchorage, AK 99504
Open Monday through Friday: 7:30 AM – 5:30 PM

Guided Tours: Scheduled weekday mornings between 9:30 AM and 11:00 AM by advance appointment to observe active classroom learning without disrupting child rest periods.

Response Time: Our administrative team typically responds to all inquiries within 1 to 2 business days.

Tour Highlights

  • Classroom Observation: See our light-filled studios, child-directed learning centers, and responsive educator interactions in real time.
  • Outdoor Meadow Walk: Explore our sensory garden, natural sand areas, and age-tailored outdoor play yards.
  • Director Dialogue: Sit down with our team to discuss your family’s routine preferences, developmental milestones, and program fit.
