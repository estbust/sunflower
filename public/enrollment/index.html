Child Privacy & Data Protection Notice

Sunflower Preschool places the highest priority on your child's digital safety and personal privacy. In accordance with our security policies, our online inquiry system strictly scans and rejects submissions containing sensitive personal data:

  • NO Medical or Health Information: Do NOT enter allergies, medical conditions, medications, clinical diagnoses, or dietary restrictions. (All health details are gathered privately in-person on confidential medical paperwork after an enrollment agreement is made.)
  • NO Full Calendar Birthdates: Do NOT enter dates formatted as MM/DD/YYYY, YYYY-MM-DD, or written birthdates. Please select a general age group only.
  • NO Government Identifiers or Financial Data: Never submit Social Security Numbers, ID numbers, or payment card information.

Important Admissions Policy: Submitting an enrollment inquiry initiates a conversation with our admissions coordinator to discuss program fit and schedule availability. It does not guarantee admission or reserve an open space in any classroom.

Admissions Pathway

What Happens After Submitting an Inquiry?

We take a relational, unhurried approach to welcoming new children and families into our community.

1 Step 1: Initial Review

Our admissions coordinator reviews your desired program cohort, start timeframe, and contact details to evaluate current classroom capacity.

2 Step 2: Campus Tour & Dialogue

We invite parents for an in-person tour during morning learning hours to observe educators, explore classroom studios, and discuss family values.

3 Step 3: Program Fit & Agreement

When a mutual match and schedule availability are confirmed, families receive a comprehensive welcome packet and complete formal in-person enrollment.
