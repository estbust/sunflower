Our Story & Philosophy

About Sunflower Preschool

Sunflower Preschool was founded on a simple, enduring conviction: childhood is not a race to be rushed, but an essential season of wonder, curiosity, and meaningful human relationships.

Life in Motion

Our Story in Motion

Take a 22-second glance inside our Anchorage classrooms to see joyful discovery, active play, and nurturing care in motion.

Our Mission

Nurturing Whole-Child Growth Through Play & Discovery

Our mission is to cultivate an inclusive, nature-rich learning community where every child feels seen, cherished, and inspired to explore their world. We believe that true learning takes root when children are invited to investigate questions that matter to them.

Through intentional materials, compassionate educator guidance, and unhurried daily rhythms, Sunflower Preschool provides an environment where toddlers, preschoolers, and school-age children develop resilience, creativity, and empathy.

Core Beliefs We Live By

  • Children are capable researchers: Young learners construct real knowledge through firsthand experimentation and play.
  • Nature is our greatest teacher: Daily outdoor discovery anchors physical health, scientific curiosity, and emotional calm.
  • Relationships precede learning: Trust, belonging, and emotional safety are the prerequisites for intellectual exploration.
  • Families are our partners: Open dialogue and mutual trust between home and school empower children to thrive.
Educational Foundation

The Four Cornerstones of The Sunflower Way

Our pedagogical approach brings together the best of progressive early childhood philosophy into practical daily practice.

Cornerstone 1

Play-Guided Inquiry

We view play as the authentic work of childhood, offering open-ended sensory and architectural materials that invite inventive thinking.

Cornerstone 2

Outdoor Stewardship

Children plant gardens, study native insects, build natural shelters, and experience weather shifts with enthusiasm and respect.

Cornerstone 3

Emotional Compassion

We teach children to identify big feelings, listen to friends, negotiate compromises, and practice kindness in everyday interactions.

Cornerstone 4

Expressive Arts

Through clay, watercolor, acoustic music, storytelling, and movement, children communicate thoughts long before formal writing begins.

Our Environment

A Campus Designed for Exploration & Joy

We consider our physical environment to be an active teacher. Every room is bathed in natural daylight, appointed with warm wood furnishings, and configured to encourage child-directed choice.

☀️ Sunlit Learning Studios

Classrooms designed with low shelving, comfortable reading corners, and flexible workspace tables where children can collaborate or work quietly.

🌿 Nature Discovery Garden

A fenced outdoor ecosystem featuring raised vegetable garden beds, sensory herb spirals, digging zones, and native pollinator plantings.

🎨 Creative Atelier

A dedicated creative studio stocked with natural clays, watercolors, easel boards, woodworking materials, and diverse textiles.
