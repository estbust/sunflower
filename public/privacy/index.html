Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy & Legal Terms

Sunflower Preschool is committed to complete transparency, respectful stewardship of family communications, and strict protection of children’s online safety.

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective for the 2026–2027 Academic Year

1 Privacy Policy & Data Safeguards

Sunflower Preschool operates with genuine respect for family privacy. This Privacy Policy details the exact types of information we receive through our digital channels, how that information is handled securely, and the concrete safeguards we maintain to protect your household and children.

Responsible Family Data Collection

When prospective or enrolled families interact with Sunflower Preschool via our website inquiry and tour scheduling forms, we collect only the minimal information strictly required to facilitate thoughtful, personalized communication:

  • Parent/Guardian Information: First and last name, primary email address, and daytime phone number.
  • Inquiry Context: Child's first name, general age bracket or program of interest (Toddler Care, Preschool, or Before/After School Care), preferred start timeframe, and general notes or questions submitted by the family.

This information is collected solely for the purposes of admissions coordination, scheduling in-person classroom tours, and responding directly to your specific questions about our program.

Secure Transmission & Anti-Spam Verification

Our website uses modern encrypted transmission protocols (HTTPS with TLS 1.3 encryption). Form submissions are securely dispatched via Web3Forms API to our verified school email (sunflowerpreschool@gci.net).

To protect our communication channels from automated spam, brute-force requests, and abuse, we implement Cloudflare Turnstile. Turnstile provides invisible, privacy-respecting bot mitigation without deploying tracking cookies, profiling users across websites, or harvesting biometric personal data.

“We NEVER sell, rent, lease, or share personal family data with any third-party marketing or commercial organizations.”

Your contact details are treated as confidential educational inquiries. We never build marketing profiles, do not participate in behavioral ad networks, and never monetize parent or guardian contact information under any circumstances.

🛡️

Strict Prohibition on Sensitive Health & Birthdate Collection Online

To safeguard the privacy of young children, our online forms strictly prohibit the submission of sensitive personal data. Our automated intake system scans for and rejects entries containing:

  • No Medical or Clinical Information: Do not enter allergies, medical conditions, medications, dietary restrictions, or developmental diagnoses online.
  • No Full Calendar Birthdates: Do not submit full birthdates (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY). We only request general age brackets.
  • No Government IDs or Financial Records: Never enter Social Security Numbers, State IDs, or payment card numbers on our website.

* Complete state-mandated health records, immunization histories, and emergency documentation are gathered exclusively in person on confidential, physical paperwork after formal enrollment has been offered and accepted.

2 Non-Discrimination Policy

Sunflower Preschool admits students of any race, color, national origin, and ethnic origin to all the rights, privileges, programs, and activities generally accorded or made available to students at the school. It does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national or ethnic origin in administration of its educational policies, admissions policies, scholarship and loan programs, and athletic and other school-administered programs.

Anchorage is one of the most culturally diverse communities in the United States, and we take deep pride in serving families from every cultural, ethnic, and socioeconomic background. Our classrooms celebrate diversity, encourage mutual empathy, and create an atmosphere where every child is valued, respected, and supported in their natural curiosity and growth.
